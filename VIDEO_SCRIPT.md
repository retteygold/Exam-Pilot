# Exam Pilot - Demo Video Script (Approx. 2–3 minutes)

## Scene 1: Intro (0:00–0:15)
**Narration:**
"Welcome to Exam Pilot — an app designed for students from LKG all the way up to Grade 12. Younger kids learn through game-style practice, and older students prepare for Cambridge O-Level, IGCSE, and AS exams using real past paper multiple-choice questions."

## Scene 2: Profile Setup (0:15–0:45)
**Show on screen:** Open `/setup`.

**Narration:**
"When a student opens the app for the first time, they complete a short profile setup: age, grade, skill level, and the exam they want to prepare for. This setup personalizes the experience."

## Scene 3: Kids Dashboard (0:45–1:20)
**Show on screen:** Select LKG or Grade 3, finish setup → Kids Dashboard.

**Narration:**
"For LKG to Grade 8, students see a kids dashboard with fun activities like Quick Quiz, Puzzle Time, Memory Match, Word Builder, Math Race, and Science Explorer. The goal is to keep learning fast, fun, and consistent — with stars, streaks, and achievements."

## Scene 4: Exam Practice (1:20–2:10)
**Show on screen:** Select Grade 10, finish setup → Home → Papers → Quiz.

**Narration:**
"For Grade 9 and above, students switch to exam practice mode. They can select papers, attempt multiple-choice questions, see results instantly, and track their progress over time in the Stats page."

## Scene 5: Admin + Verification (2:10–2:40)
**Show on screen:** `/admin` → open a question → show PDF page image viewer.

**Narration:**
"For content management, the Admin page allows adding and editing questions, uploading images, and verifying against PDF page images saved in the project."

## Scene 6: Extraction Pipeline (2:40–3:00)
**Show on screen:** `extract.md` quickly.

**Narration:**
"Finally, Exam Pilot includes an extraction pipeline that converts Cambridge past papers into structured JSON and generates upload-ready files for Supabase. Full instructions are documented in the extraction guide."

## Closing (3:00)
**Narration:**
"That’s Exam Pilot — kids learn with games, older students prepare with real past papers, and admins can continuously expand the question bank."
