# Exam Pilot — TikTok Promo Script (Ready to Record)

## Video Goal
Show that Exam Pilot makes studying feel like a game for kids + serious exam practice for older students.

## Target length
- 20–30 seconds

## Format
- Fast cuts
- Screen recording + 1–2 talking-head clips (optional)
- Captions on every scene

---

## Script (30s)

### 0:00–0:02 — Hook (big promise)
**On-screen text:** “Kids bored of studying? Try this.”
**Voiceover:** “If your child hates revision, this turns it into a game.”

### 0:02–0:06 — Show Kids Dashboard (bright UI)
**Shot:** Open app → Kids Dashboard
**On-screen text:** “LKG–Grade 8: Games + rewards”
**Voiceover:** “For LKG to Grade 8, they get learning games…”

**Quick cut (optional):** Kids login screen
**On-screen text:** “Login: Code • Email • Google”

### 0:06–0:12 — Show 2–3 games quickly
**Shots (fast):**
- Tap **Quiz Race** → answer a question → streak popup
- Tap **Speed Challenge** → timer running
- Tap **Word Search** → find a word

**On-screen text:** “Streaks • Stars • Popups • Sounds”
**Voiceover:** “They earn stars, streaks, and reward popups—with sound effects.”

### 0:12–0:16 — Show rewards moment (the “wow”)
**Shot:** Trigger a win popup (confetti)
**On-screen text:** “Achievement unlocked!”
**Voiceover:** “So they actually *want* to keep going.”

### 0:16–0:22 — Show older students flow (serious)
**Shots:** Switch to Grade 9–12 → Papers → Quiz → Results
**On-screen text:** “Grade 9–12: Past paper MCQs”
**Voiceover:** “And for Grade 9 to 12, it’s real Cambridge past-paper MCQ practice.”

### 0:22–0:27 — Trust + progress
**Shot:** Stats page / results summary
**On-screen text:** “Track progress”
**Voiceover:** “You can track accuracy and improvement over time.”

**Quick cut (optional):** Kids leaderboard
**On-screen text:** “Leaderboards + flags”

### 0:27–0:30 — CTA
**On-screen text:** “Want the link? Comment ‘EXAM’”
**Voiceover:** “Want the link? Comment ‘EXAM’ and I’ll send it.”
